**Technical Specification: Two-Bit Saturating Counter**

**1. Introduction**

This specification defines a two-bit saturating counter module. The counter's behavior is governed by the `train_valid` and `train_taken` inputs. It increments when both are asserted, decrements when `train_valid` is asserted and `train_taken` is deasserted, and holds its value when `train_valid` is deasserted. The counter saturates at 0 and 3. An asynchronous reset (`areset`) initializes the counter to 1 (weakly not-taken).

**2. Functional Requirements**

*   **Clocked Operation:** The counter operates synchronously with the positive edge of the `clk` signal.
*   **Asynchronous Reset:** The `areset` signal is a positive-edge triggered asynchronous reset. When asserted, the counter is reset to the value 1 (2'b01).
*   **Increment:** When `train_valid` and `train_taken` are both asserted (1), the counter increments.
*   **Decrement:** When `train_valid` is asserted (1) and `train_taken` is deasserted (0), the counter decrements.
*   **Hold:** When `train_valid` is deasserted (0), the counter holds its current value.
*   **Saturation:**
    *   The counter saturates at 0 (2'b00). Decrementing from 0 results in 0.
    *   The counter saturates at 3 (2'b11). Incrementing from 3 results in 3.
*   **Output:** The `state[1:0]` output reflects the current value of the counter.
