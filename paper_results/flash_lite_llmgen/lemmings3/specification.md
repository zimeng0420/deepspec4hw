**Technical Specification: Lemmings Finite State Machine**

**1.  Introduction**

This specification details the design of a Moore state machine that models the behavior of a Lemming in a 2D environment. The Lemming can walk left or right, change direction upon bumping into obstacles, fall when there's no ground, dig through the ground, and perform these actions based on the provided inputs.

**2.  Inputs and Outputs (as defined in the module header)**

*   **Inputs:**
    *   `clk`:  Clock signal (used for synchronous state updates).
    *   `areset`: Asynchronous reset signal (active high). Resets the Lemming to the `WALK_LEFT` state.
    *   `bump_left`:  Indicates a bump on the left side (1 = bump).
    *   `bump_right`: Indicates a bump on the right side (1 = bump).
    *   `ground`: Indicates the presence of ground (1 = ground, 0 = no ground).
    *   `dig`:  Indicates the command to start digging (1 = dig).
*   **Outputs:**
    *   `walk_left`:  Indicates the Lemming is walking left (1 = walking left).
    *   `walk_right`: Indicates the Lemming is walking right (1 = walking right).
    *   `aaah`:  Indicates the Lemming is falling (1 = falling).
    *   `digging`: Indicates the Lemming is digging (1 = digging).

**3.  States**

The state machine will have the following states:

*   `IDLE`: The initial state, or the state after reset.
*   `WALK_LEFT`: The Lemming is walking left.
*   `WALK_RIGHT`: The Lemming is walking right.
*   `FALLING`: The Lemming is falling.
*   `DIGGING`: The Lemming is digging.
