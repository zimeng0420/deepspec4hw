**2. Functional Description:**

The `top_module` implements a 4-bit right-shift register with the following functionality:

*   **Asynchronous Reset (`areset`):** When `areset` is asserted (high), the shift register `q` is immediately reset to zero, regardless of the clock signal. This reset is asynchronous, meaning it doesn't wait for a clock edge.
*   **Load (`load`):** When `load` is asserted (high) and the clock rises, the shift register `q` is loaded with the value of the input `data[3:0]`.
*   **Enable (`ena`):** When `ena` is asserted (high) and the clock rises, the shift register `q` shifts its contents one bit to the right. The most significant bit (MSB, `q[3]`) is set to zero, and the least significant bit (LSB, `q[0]`) is shifted out and lost.
*   **Priority:** If both `load` and `ena` are asserted simultaneously at the rising edge of the clock, the `load` signal takes precedence. The register will load the data instead of shifting.
*   **Clocked Operation:** All operations (load and shift) are performed on the rising edge of the `clk` signal.
