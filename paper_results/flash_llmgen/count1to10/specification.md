**Technical Specification: Decade Counter (1-10) with Synchronous Reset**

**1. Introduction**

This document specifies the design and implementation of a decade counter that counts from 1 to 10 (inclusive). The counter utilizes a synchronous active-high reset signal to reset the count to 1. The counter is implemented in Verilog.

**2. Functional Description**

The decade counter increments its output `q` on each rising edge of the clock signal `clk`. The counter counts from 1 to 10. After reaching 10, the counter wraps around to 1 on the next clock cycle.  The counter also has a synchronous reset input. When the `reset` input is asserted (high) on a rising edge of the `clk`, the counter's output `q` is immediately set to 1.
