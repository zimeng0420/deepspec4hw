**2. Functional Description**

The full adder performs the following arithmetic operation:

`sum = a XOR b XOR cin`

*   **Sum:** The sum bit is 1 if an odd number of the inputs (a, b, cin) are 1.
*   **Carry-out:** The carry-out bit is 1 if at least two of the inputs (a, b, cin) are 1.
