3.  **BYTE1 State:** The FSM has received the first byte of a potential message. It transitions to the `BYTE2` state in the next clock cycle.

4.  **BYTE2 State:** The FSM has received the second byte of a potential message. It transitions to the `BYTE3` state in the next clock cycle.

5.  **BYTE3 State:** The FSM has received the third byte of a potential message.
    *   The `done` signal is asserted (set to 1) in this state.
    *   In the next clock cycle, the FSM transitions back to the `IDLE` state, and the `done` signal is de-asserted (set to 0).

6.  **Data Validity:** The FSM does *not* validate the data content of the bytes. It only checks `in[3]` for the first byte and counts the subsequent two bytes.

7.  **Timing:** The `done` signal is asserted for one clock cycle immediately after the third byte is received.

8.  **Synchronous Reset:** The reset is synchronous, meaning the FSM will only reset on the rising edge of the clock signal when the `reset` signal is asserted.
