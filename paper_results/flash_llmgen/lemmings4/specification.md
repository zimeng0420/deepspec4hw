**Technical Specification: Lemmings FSM**

**1. Introduction**

This document specifies the design of a Moore-type Finite State Machine (FSM) that models the behavior of a Lemming in a simplified 2D environment. The Lemming can walk left or right, fall, dig, and splatter. The FSM is implemented in Verilog.

**2. Functional Requirements**

The FSM must implement the following behaviors:

*   **Walking:** The Lemming walks either left or right. It starts walking left after a reset.
*   **Direction Change:** The Lemming changes direction when bumped on the left or right.  If bumped on both sides simultaneously, it still changes direction.
*   **Falling:** When `ground` is 0, the Lemming falls and outputs "aaah!".  The walking direction is preserved during the fall.
*   **Digging:** When `dig` is 1 and the Lemming is walking on the ground, it starts digging. It continues digging until `ground` becomes 0 (reaches the other side). Digging is ignored if the Lemming is falling or there is no ground.
*   **Splattering:** If the Lemming falls for more than 20 clock cycles and then hits the ground, it splatters.  When splattered, all outputs are set to 0, and the FSM remains in this state indefinitely until reset.
*   **Precedence:** Fall has higher precedence than dig, which has higher precedence than switching directions.

**4. States**

*   `WALK_LEFT`: Lemming is walking left.
*   `WALK_RIGHT`: Lemming is walking right.
*   `FALL_LEFT`: Lemming is falling while previously walking left.
*   `FALL_RIGHT`: Lemming is falling while previously walking right.
*   `DIG_LEFT`: Lemming is digging while previously walking left.
*   `DIG_RIGHT`: Lemming is digging while previously walking right.
*   `SPLATTERED`: Lemming has splattered and is no longer active.
