Design Description**

The 4-bit adder is constructed using four full adders connected in a cascade configuration.  A full adder takes three inputs: two bits to be added (`A` and `B`) and a carry-in bit (`Cin`). It produces two outputs: a sum bit (`S`) and a carry-out bit (`Cout`).

*   **Full Adder Implementation:**  Each full adder implements the following logic:

    *   `S = A XOR B XOR Cin`
    *   `Cout = (A AND B) OR (A AND Cin) OR (B AND Cin)`

*   **Cascading Full Adders:** The carry-out of each full adder is connected to the carry-in of the next higher-order full adder.  The carry-in of the least significant bit (LSB) full adder is set to 0.

*   **Overflow Detection:** The overflow bit is determined by examining the carry-in and carry-out of the most significant bit (MSB) full adder.  Overflow occurs if the carry-in and carry-out of the MSB full adder are different.  This can be expressed as:

    *   `Overflow = Cin_MSB XOR Cout_MSB`
