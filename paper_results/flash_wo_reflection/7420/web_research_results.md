**Functionality:**

*   Each of the four NAND gates has four inputs (A, B, C, and D) and one output (Y).
*   A NAND gate produces an output that is the inverse (NOT) of the AND operation.
*   The output Y is logic HIGH (1) in all cases except when all four inputs A, B, C, and D are logic HIGH (1), in which case Y is logic LOW (0).
*   The equation A * B * C * D = X (inverted) describes the relationship between inputs and output.

**Key Features:**

*   The 7420 IC contains two independent 4-input NAND gates.
*   It typically uses CMOS technology, offering low power consumption and high noise immunity.
*   It is part of the 7400 series, ensuring compatibility with other TTL or CMOS logic ICs.
*   It operates in a voltage range of 4.5V to 5.5V and handles logic-level inputs and outputs.

**Applications:**

*   The 7420 IC is often used in general-purpose digital logic circuits.
*   NAND gates are used for creating combinational logic circuits, such as adders, multiplexers, and decoders.
*   They are also used in switching circuits to select between different signal paths based on control inputs.
