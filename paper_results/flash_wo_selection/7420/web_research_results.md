## Result 1

The 7420 chip is a member of the 7400 series of integrated circuits. These ICs are a popular logic family of transistor-transistor logic (TTL). The 7420 specifically contains two independent 4-input NAND gates.

Here's what that means:

*   **7400 Series:** A broad line of digital logic integrated circuits.
*   **NAND Gate:** A logic gate that produces a LOW (0) output only when all its inputs are HIGH (1); otherwise, the output is HIGH (1). It performs the inverse of the AND operation. NAND gates are considered universal gates because they can be used to implement any other type of logic gate.
*   **Dual:** The 7420 has two independent NAND gates within the single IC package.
*   **4-Input:** Each NAND gate has four input pins.

Each of the NAND gates in a 7420 has four inputs and one output. The remaining pins are for power (+5V) and ground.
