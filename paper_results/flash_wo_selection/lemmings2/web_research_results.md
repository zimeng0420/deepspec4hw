## Result 1

The game Lemmings involves guiding creatures with simple AI through a 2D world. Lemmings walk left or right and change direction upon hitting an obstacle.

**Core Movement and Obstacle Interaction:**

*   **Walking:** Lemmings walk at a steady pace horizontally or on moderate slopes. They cannot stand still unless designated as blockers.
*   **Direction Change:** When a walker encounters a wall or obstacle, it reverses direction.
*   **Cliffs:** Walkers fall off cliffs. The height of the fall determines if they survive.
*   **Climbing:** Climbers can climb vertical walls.
*   **Falling:** Lemmings can fall from cliffs. Falling from too great a height can be fatal. Floaters can fall safely from any height.

**Lemmings Skills and Abilities:**

*   **Walker:** Walks either left or right, reverses direction when hitting a wall.
*   **Climber:** Climbs vertical walls.
*   **Floater:** Can fall safely from any height.
*   **Blocker:** Stands still, causing other lemmings to turn around.
*   **Bomber:** Explodes after a 5-second countdown, destroying surrounding material.
*   **Builder:** Builds a bridge diagonally upwards.
*   **Basher:** Digs a horizontal hole through walls.
*   **Miner:** Digs diagonally downwards.
*   **Digger:** Digs vertically down.

**Additional Considerations:**

*   The Lemmings world has kinematics but not dynamics.
*   The game is deterministic; the same actions in identical situations will always lead to the same outcome.
*   Levels have a time limit.
*   The game presents challenges in AI due to the fast-paced environment and the need for qualitative and partial knowledge of the game's rules.
*   The complexity of Lemmings makes creating an AI to solve levels difficult, with the game being NP-hard even under restricted rules.
