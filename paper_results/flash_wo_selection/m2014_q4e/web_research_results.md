## Result 1

A NOR gate is a digital logic gate that implements logical NOR. It produces a HIGH output (1) only if all its inputs are LOW (0); otherwise, the output is LOW (0). It is the negation of the OR operator. NOR gates are fundamental in constructing logical circuits and are used in both combinational and sequential logic circuits. They can be used to create other logic gate functions, making them universal gates.

A 2-input NOR gate can be described as follows:

*   It has two inputs.
*   It produces an output that is normally at logic level "1" and only goes "LOW" to logic level "0" when ANY of its inputs are at logic level "1".
*   The Boolean expression for a NOR gate is A+B = Q (where the line over the expression signifies the NOT or logical negation).

NOR gates simplify circuit design and enhance reliability. They are also used in digital comparators, memory circuits, latches, flip-flop designs, alarm circuits, and safety systems.
