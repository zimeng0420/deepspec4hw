## Result 1

The design of a ringer/vibrator control circuit for a cellphone involves activating either the ringer or the vibration motor based on the phone's mode. The circuit should not activate both simultaneously. When the phone is in vibrate mode, the motor should be activated; otherwise, the ringer should be activated upon an incoming call.

Several aspects are important in the design of such a circuit:

**Basic Functionality:** The circuit needs to detect an incoming call and then activate either the ringer or vibrator based on the "vibrate_mode" input.

**Vibration Motor Control:**
*   A simple MOSFET switch can control the power delivered to the vibration motor.
*   A ceramic capacitor placed close to the motor terminals can help remove high-frequency noise. Sometimes, mobile phones have another capacitor connected to the ground on each motor terminal for further noise filtering.
*   Using a PWM signal to drive the motor requires careful consideration of capacitor values to avoid filtering frequencies above the PWM frequency.
*   Techniques like reverse braking can stop the motor quickly by reversing the polarity of the supply voltage until the motor stops.
*   Adding chokes can combat common-mode current noise, especially when the motor is located far from the drive circuitry.
*   Flyback diodes (snubbing diodes) can protect the motor drive circuitry from voltage spikes generated by the motor.

**Implementation Considerations:**

*   In real-world scenarios, ready-made vibrator motors are readily available. These typically have an unbalanced weight on the output shaft to produce vibrations.
*   The circuit can be used for customized applications.
*   An on/off switch can help prevent unnecessary battery drain.
*   A piezo sensor can detect vibrations and convert them into electrical signals. This signal can then be amplified using a transistorized amplifier.
*   A capacitor can stabilize the relay switching if the vibration rate is inconsistent.

The module should have the following interface:

```verilog
module top_module(
    input ring,
    input vibrate_mode,
    output ringer,
    output motor
);
```

Here:

*   `ring` indicates an incoming call.
*   `vibrate_mode` indicates whether the phone is in vibrate mode.
*   `ringer` is the output signal to activate the ringer.
*   `motor` is the output signal to activate the vibration motor.
