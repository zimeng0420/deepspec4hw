## Result 1

AND and OR gates are fundamental building blocks in digital circuit design. They perform logical operations on one or more binary inputs, producing a single binary output. These gates are essential for implementing Boolean functions and creating complex digital systems.

**AND Gate:**
*   The AND gate performs logical multiplication (conjunction) of its inputs.
*   The output of an AND gate is HIGH (1 or true) only when all its inputs are HIGH (1 or true). If any input is LOW (0 or false), the output is LOW (0 or false).
*   The AND operation can be represented by a dot (·) or the symbol "^" or "&". For example, C = A ⋅ B.
*   AND gates can have multiple inputs.

**OR Gate:**
*   The OR gate performs logical addition (disjunction) of its inputs.
*   The output of an OR gate is HIGH (1 or true) if any of its inputs is HIGH (1 or true). The output is LOW (0 or false) only when all inputs are LOW (0 or false).
*   The OR operation is represented by a plus sign (+). For example, X = A + B.
*   OR gates can have multiple inputs.

**Role in Digital Circuit Design:**
*   Logic gates, including AND and OR gates, are used to perform logical operations based on Boolean algebra.
*   By combining different logic gates, complex operations can be performed, and more complex circuits can be designed, such as flip-flops, counters, processors, multiplexers, arithmetic logic units (ALUs), and computer memory.
*   AND and OR gates are used in both combinational and sequential logic circuits.
*   They are used in digital electronics in devices such as smartphones, tablets, and memory devices.
*   Along with the NOT gate, the AND and OR gates are the three basic logic gates from which any Boolean circuit may be constructed.
