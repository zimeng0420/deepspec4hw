## Result 1

A half adder is a digital logic circuit that adds two single-bit binary numbers. It produces two outputs: the sum (S) and the carry-out (Cout).

**Practical Applications:**

*   **Arithmetic Logic Units (ALUs):** Half adders are used within the ALU of computer processors to perform binary addition.
*   **Full Adder Construction:** Half adders serve as fundamental building blocks for constructing full adders, which can handle a carry-in from previous stages. A full adder can be implemented using two half adders.
*   **Calculators:** Half adders are utilized in the design of calculators.
*   **Address Decoding:** They are used in memory address decoding circuits to generate specific memory locations.
*   **Data Processing:** Half adders find applications in digital signal processing, data encryption, and error correction.
*   **Other Applications:** Half adders are also used in encoder and decoder circuits, multiplexers and demultiplexers and counters.

**Working Principle:**

A half adder consists of an XOR gate and an AND gate. The XOR gate produces the SUM output, while the AND gate produces the CARRY output. The inputs to the half adder are the two bits to be added (A and B).

**Limitations:**

A half adder does not have a carry-in input, which limits its use in multi-bit addition. For multi-bit addition, full adders are used.
